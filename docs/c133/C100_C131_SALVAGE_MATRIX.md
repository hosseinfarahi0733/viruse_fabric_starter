## Decision rule

An artifact is retained only if it contributes a proved mathematical result,
a faithful semantic primitive, an independent executable check, or a rigorous
falsification/reproducibility practice. Similar terminology alone is not a
bridge.

1. `ThreeTimeChainRepresentabilityCharacterization.lean` is imported by the
   new module.

   executable natural-defect theorem.
3. the parity witness supplies both strict observer refinement and a non-chain
   defect fixture.

   recreated for the new semantics.
5. C127-style direct Lean fixtures, axiom audit, full Lake build, forbidden
   token scan and archive checks are retained.

## Explicitly not salvaged as science

- arbitrary causal-mass, gravity, curvature and apparent-targeting weights;
- three physical time dimensions;
- product ledger update as viral dynamics;
- token/window reentry as a proxy for tri-temporal causation;
- empirical conclusions from internal toy fixtures.
